# NEXARQ — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-11 · **App version:** 1.0.0

NEXARQ ("the app") is designed to be privacy-first. This policy explains what the
app does and does not do with your data.

## 1. Data processed locally

All file-management and archive operations happen **on your device**. The app reads
your files only to perform the actions you request (browse, copy, move, compress,
extract, hash, analyze, preview, edit).

The app does **not** upload your files or file listings to any server.

## 2. What is stored

- **App settings** (theme, sorting, toggles) — stored locally.
- **Favorites/bookmarks and recent files** — stored locally; you can disable recent
  history in Settings.
- **AI API keys** (only if you choose to add them) — stored encrypted in the Android
  Keystore and never written to logs or backups in plaintext.
- **AI chat history** (only if you use the AI Center) — stored locally and can be
  cleared at any time. API keys are never part of chat history.

## 3. Network use

The app uses the network **only** when you:

- Configure and enable an AI provider (Google Gemini and/or OpenRouter), and
- Explicitly send content (a prompt, and optionally file text you include).

When you use an AI provider, the request and any content you include are sent to that
provider's servers under **their** terms and privacy policies. Usage may incur
charges on your provider account. You are in control: AI is disabled by default, you
choose what to send, and you can remove API keys at any time.

## 4. What we do NOT do

- No analytics SDK, no crash reporting SDK, no advertising SDK.
- No hidden telemetry or usage tracking.
- No collection of your personal information.
- No sale or sharing of data.

## 5. Permissions

- **Notifications** — only to show progress for long operations you start.
- **Internet** — only for the optional AI features.
- **Manage external storage** — declared only as a *feature* to detect capable
  devices; the app uses Storage Access Framework / scoped storage and does not
  require broad storage access.

## 6. Root mode

If your device is rooted and you enable root mode, the app can read and modify system
files. These actions are always explicit, require confirmation for dangerous
operations, and are performed only with your consent.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no personal information.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published with the app.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the repository maintainer.
